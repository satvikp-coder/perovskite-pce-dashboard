- Source: The Perovskite Database Project, https://www.perovskitedatabase.com
- Reference: Jacobsson, T. J. et al. "An open-access database and analysis tool for perovskite solar cells based on the FAIR data principles." Nature Energy 7, 107-115 (2022). doi:10.1038/s41560-021-00941-3 (online 2021). Bibliographic details checked against the Nature Energy page.
